# Prompt video 15 detik — Wan 3.0

**Delapan prompt × 15 detik = 2 menit.** Koreografi, peta klip, dan tabel
pukulannya sama persis dengan
[`VIDEO-CHINATSU-VS-HINA-15-SEEDANCE.md`](VIDEO-CHINATSU-VS-HINA-15-SEEDANCE.md)
— baca peta klip di sana. Gambar acuan dan prompt kondisinya ada di
[`VIDEO-CHINATSU-VS-HINA.md`](VIDEO-CHINATSU-VS-HINA.md).

Yang berbeda cuma pembungkusnya: `Image N is Boxer X`, `Shot N [0-3s]`
dengan kurung siku, baris `Sound:` sendiri di tiap shot, 30 fps, dan blok
penutup yang panjang-panjang.

Ada satu blok baru yang tidak ada di versi 30 detik: **Boxing craft**.
Isinya tiga aturan tetap — gerak kaki, reaksi kena pukul, dan cairan —
dipisahkan dari `Animation craft` supaya jelas mana arahan tinju dan mana
arahan menggambar. Blok itu sama di kedelapan prompt; salin apa adanya.

| # | Detik | Isi | Acuan |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–15 | Ronde 1 dibuka, saling mengukur, jab perut pertama | C0 + H0 |
| 2 | 15–30 | Rentetan Hina ke wajah lalu hook ke samping perut | C1 + H1 |
| 3 | 30–45 | Uppercut ke dagu, hook kanan, Chinatsu jatuh | C1 + H1 |
| 4 | 45–60 | **Interval** — menyender di sudut ring | C1 + H1 |
| 5 | 60–75 | Ronde 2, counter straight, straight ke ulu hati | C1 + H1 |
| 6 | 75–90 | Hook liver, uppercut ke perut, hook kanan ke kepala | C1 + H2 |
| 7 | 90–105 | Terdesak di tali ring, shovel hook, Chinatsu mengayun | C2 + H3 |
| 8 | 105–120 | Ducking, counter uppercut ke dagu, kombinasi, KO | C2 + H3 |

## Klip 1 — Ronde 1, saling mengukur · acuan C0 + H0

```
Generate a 15-second 16:9 video at 30fps: an anime boxing match, modern digital TV anime, thin tapered lineart, two-tone cel shading, glossy highlights.

Image 1 is Boxer A — light brown hair to the jaw with blunt bangs, brown eyes, a cropped yellow short-sleeved gym shirt with white trim, white dolphin shorts with pale blue piping, white hand wraps, and bright red boxing gloves with white cuffs. Orthodox stance, 162cm, a clear head taller with the longer reach, heavier hands.
Image 2 is Boxer B — pink hair with a low bun on each side and blunt bangs, pink eyes, a loose cropped white short-sleeved t-shirt worn over a navy blue high-leg one-piece swimsuit, white hand wraps, and bright blue boxing gloves with white cuffs. Orthodox stance, 150cm, twelve centimetres shorter, faster hands, works from underneath.
Image 3 is the venue — no fighter in it, read the place only.
Image 4 is the art style — no character in it, read the drawing look only.

Shot 1 [0-3s]: The bell. Both boxers push off their corner posts and begin working the canvas in small measuring steps. A canvas-level shot at ankle height with both pairs of shoes filling the foreground, the camera rising slowly to a low wide.
Sound: one clean bell, then shoes finding the canvas in an empty hall.

Shot 2 [3-6s]: Boxer A (Image 1) steps in behind a long left jab; Boxer B (Image 2) ducks under it, dropping her whole level so the glove passes over her hair, and comes back up on the outside of it. A shot from outside the ropes with the top rope cutting across the frame, the camera drifting sideways.
Sound: leather cutting air, touching nothing.

Shot 3 [6-9s]: Boxer A doubles the jab; Boxer B sways backward from the waist and both punches fall an inch short of her face, then she rocks straight back in. An over-the-shoulder from behind Boxer B with her blue glove blurred in the foreground, the camera holding still.
Sound: two sharp exhales and the creak of a shoe turning.

Shot 4 [9-12s]: Pure footwork — Boxer B circles left without ever squaring up, feet crossing and uncrossing, while Boxer A pivots to follow and is always a beat late. A low rake across the canvas at ankle height, the camera tracking laterally with the floor reflections streaking under them.
Sound: quick light steps against slower heavier ones.

Shot 5 [12-15s]: Boxer B slips inside and taps a short left jab into Boxer A's stomach — the first punch to land all night — and is gone before the hands come down. Boxer A's stomach flinches, a small huff of air escapes her, and sweat jumps off her shoulders. A medium shot that racks focus from Boxer A's guard in the foreground to Boxer B sharpening behind it.
Sound: one soft flat thud into the body, then an involuntary grunt.

Condition in this clip: both fighters are clean and unmarked. Boxer A starts breathing easily and is damp and working harder by the end; Boxer B is barely warm and does not look troubled at any point.

Boxing craft: both box like trained professionals, not brawlers. They slip, duck, sway back from the waist, roll under hooks, pivot off the lead foot and circle constantly — nobody stands square and trades. Reaction rule: a shot to the head snaps the neck, deforms the cheek around the glove and unfocuses the eyes for a beat; a shot to the stomach does the opposite — the body jackknifes forward, the mouth stretches open with no sound, the eyes go wide and the guard drops. Fluid rule: every clean impact throws sweat off in discrete droplets; a shot to the stomach forces out a spray of saliva along with the air; a fine dark-red spray appears only once a fighter is heavily bruised, and neither of them is yet.

Throughout the whole clip: strictly lock every character to their reference image — hair colour, eye colour, glove colour and outfit must not change at any point. Boxer A's gloves are red and Boxer B's are blue, and they never swap. Boxer A stays on the right side of frame, Boxer B stays on the left. Lock the height difference as hard as the glove colour: Boxer A stands a clear head taller, 162cm against 150cm, Boxer B's eyes level with her chin, and she throws upward. Never draw them the same height. Every movement stays physically possible, with weight and follow-through. Cut fast — five distinct shots in fifteen seconds, every cut landing on a movement. No background music.

Scene: The venue is Image 3 — a school gymnasium at night. A full competition ring stands raised on a platform in the middle of a polished wooden basketball court: blue canvas, red, white and blue ropes, blue corner posts on the left and red corner posts on the right. Basketball backboards hang on the side walls, tall windows show a dark blue night sky, and warm overhead lamps throw long reflections down the wooden floor. No audience at all — every seat and every inch of floor is empty. No referee and no corner team in frame.

Art style: Image 4 is the art-style reference — take only the drawing look from it, never a character. Modern digital TV anime: thin tapered lineart, two-tone cel shading with hard shadow edges, glossy specular highlights on the gloves and hair, a warm rim light from overhead separating the figures from a near-black background, and sweat drawn as individual droplets.

Animation craft: this is hand-drawn Japanese animation, not filmed footage. Time the movement unevenly — hold poses on twos while they circle, then burst into full framerate for two or three frames on every punch. Put a single white impact frame on each clean connection, radiating speed lines from the point of contact, and let the fastest part of a swing become a smear frame rather than a sharp arm. Keep strong anticipation before each punch and heavy follow-through after it, with hair and flesh lagging a frame behind the bone. Cel-shaded flat colour with hard shadow edges; no motion blur on the characters themselves, only on the background during fast camera moves.
```
