# Prompt video — Wan 3.0

Empat prompt, masing-masing berdiri sendiri. Gambar acuan dan prompt
kondisinya ada di [`VIDEO-CHINATSU-VS-HINA.md`](VIDEO-CHINATSU-VS-HINA.md).

Ciri khas penulisan Wan yang dipakai di sini: `Image N is Boxer X`,
`Shot N [0-3s]` dengan kurung siku, baris `Sound:` sendiri di tiap shot,
30 fps, dan empat blok penutup panjang (`Throughout` / `Scene` /
`Art style` / `Animation craft`). Jangan dicampur dengan penulisan
Seedance — keduanya membaca struktur yang berbeda.

```

Shot 6 [15-18s]: Boxer B steps in on the angle and lands a clean one-two on Boxer A's face; the head snaps back on the second one. An extreme close-up on the point of impact, the impact playing in slow motion.
Sound: two cracks, the second flatter and heavier.

Shot 7 [18-21s]: Boxer A pulls her gloves tight to her head and Boxer B works all around the guard — shots landing on the arms, the shoulders, over the top. A medium shot pushing in, handheld with slight shake.

Shot 8 [21-24s]: Boxer A lunges forward to take the fight back; Boxer B pivots off her lead foot, opens the angle and puts a left hook on the side of her jaw. A single white impact frame flashes on contact, speed lines burst outward, and a one-frame freeze lands on the connection before the recoil begins. A Dutch-angled medium shot, the horizon tilted hard.
Sound: one sharp crack, and the air going out of the room.

Shot 9 [24-27s]: Boxer A's legs fold under her and she drops onto the canvas, one glove skidding out to break the fall. The impact ripples visibly through her body, hair and flesh lagging a frame behind the bone. A whip pan down following her to the canvas.
Sound: a body hitting canvas, the ropes shivering.

Shot 10 [27-30s]: Boxer A pushes herself up onto one knee, breathing hard, a first red mark rising on her left cheekbone, and looks across at Boxer B with her jaw set. Boxer B waits in the far corner barely marked, shaking out her arms. A low wide shot, the camera pulling back slowly.
Sound: one boxer's ragged breathing, the empty gym swallowing it.

Condition in this clip: Boxer A (Image 1) starts clean and unmarked and ends soaked in sweat, wet hair stuck to her face, with one fresh red mark coming up on her left cheekbone — no blood anywhere yet. Boxer B (Image 2) stays unmarked for the whole clip; the only thing that changes in her is sweat and a faster rise and fall of her chest. Boxer B never looks tired. Boxer A's breathing gets visibly worse from shot 5 onward, and after shot 9 her eyes take two seconds to focus again.

Throughout the whole clip: strictly lock every character to their reference image — hair colour, eye colour, glove colour and outfit must not change at any point. Boxer A's gloves are red and Boxer B's are blue, and they never swap. Keep the screen direction consistent: Boxer A (Image 1) stays on the right side of frame, Boxer B (Image 2) stays on the left. Lock the height difference as hard as the glove colour: Boxer A stands a clear head taller than Boxer B, 162cm against 150cm, and that gap must read in every single two-shot — Boxer B's eyes sit level with Boxer A's chin, Boxer B punches upward on every shot she throws, and Boxer A has to bend her knees to reach Boxer B's body. Never draw them the same height. Only the two boxers are clearly readable. Every movement stays physically possible, with weight and follow-through. Cut fast and often — average shot length around three seconds, every cut landing on a movement rather than between them. No background music.

Scene: The venue is Image 3 — a school gymnasium at night. A full competition ring stands raised on a platform in the middle of a polished wooden basketball court: blue canvas, red, white and blue ropes, blue corner posts on the left and red corner posts on the right, a navy skirt around the platform. No audience at all — every seat and every inch of floor is empty, so each impact and each breath echoes off bare walls. No referee and no corner second in frame.

Modern digital TV anime: thin tapered lineart, two-tone cel shading with hard shadow edges, glossy specular highlights on the gloves and hair, a warm rim light from overhead separating the figures from a near-black background, soft airbrushed blush across cheeks and shoulders, and sweat drawn as individual droplets.

Animation craft: this is hand-drawn Japanese animation, not filmed footage. Time the movement unevenly — hold poses on twos while they circle, then burst into full framerate for two or three frames on every punch. Put a single white impact frame on each clean connection, radiating speed lines from the point of contact, and let the fastest part of a swing become a smear frame rather than a sharp arm. Sweat flies off in discrete droplets, not a spray. Keep strong anticipation before each punch and heavy follow-through after it, with hair and flesh lagging a frame behind the bone. Cel-shaded flat colour with hard shadow edges throughout; no motion blur on the characters themselves, only on the background during fast camera moves.
```

## Kalau 30 detik ditolak

Tiap klip bisa dipotong dua di detik 15 tanpa merusak apa pun: shot 1–5
jadi satu generasi, shot 6–10 jadi generasi berikutnya dengan timestamp
digeser ke 0. Empat blok penutupnya disalin apa adanya ke kedua belahan,
dan baris `Condition` disesuaikan — belahan pertama memakai wujud awal,
belahan kedua memakai wujud akhir. Hasilnya delapan klip 15 detik dengan
cerita yang persis sama.
